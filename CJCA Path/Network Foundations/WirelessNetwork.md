1. What type of waves do wireless networks use to connect devices? (Format: two words)\
Ans: radio waves

2. What device combines the functions of routing and providing Wi-Fi coverage in a home network? (Format: two words)\
Ans: wireless router

3. What is used by a mobile hotspot to connect devices to the internet? (Format: two words)\
Ans: cellular data

4. What structure supports antennas and communications equipment to create cellular network coverage? (Format: two words)\
Ans: cell tower

5. What manages multiple cell towers in cellular networks? (Format: three words)\
Ans: Base Station Controller
